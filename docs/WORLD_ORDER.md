# World Order 03 — Politics, Powers, Military, Institutions, Artifacts, Economy and Daily Life

**Status: Working worldbuilding framework — author review required.**

This document proposes the social and institutional order of the Beast Taming world. It makes the setting a functioning civilization rather than a collection of combat arenas. The seven elemental families are major powers, but they do not automatically own every institution or govern every person.

## Canon discipline

**Established foundations:** the world has seven major elemental families; the Dark Family was betrayed and is constrained by a promise made by a former head; demands from the other six contribute to its decline; its ancestral knowledge allows its members to cultivate beasts from all seven elements; mana, mana-bearing materials, beasts, plants, cores and planetary mana veins exist.

**Working proposals:** the government, political bodies, military branches, academy network, artifact classifications, currency, market rules and social institutions below. Future chapter writers must not treat proposals as immutable canon until the author confirms them.

---

## 1. The shape of civilization

### Recommended model: an imperial realm with seven great hereditary houses

The most flexible structure is a large political realm ruled by a central sovereign authority, alongside seven ancient elemental families whose influence rivals or predates the current dynasty.

The sovereign government provides common law, taxation, roads, city administration, border defence and coordination during large-scale crises. The seven families retain hereditary territories, private resources, beast-breeding grounds, cultivation traditions and family forces, subject to imperial law and political agreements.

Three kinds of authority overlap:

1. **Territorial authority:** the sovereign state and appointed governors administer cities, towns, roads, farms and public lands.
2. **Hereditary authority:** the seven great families control ancestral estates, specialist resources, family towns, private beast habitats and inherited techniques.
3. **Institutional authority:** academies, merchant associations, beast-tamer guilds, mercenary companies and religious or charitable institutions operate across family borders.

No group has unlimited power. A family may dominate a region but still need imperial recognition, trade access, academy graduates and alliances. The throne may issue laws but cannot destroy all seven families without risking civil war and the collapse of essential services.

This creates several scales for the protagonist's journey: household, family, city, regional politics, imperial institutions and eventually the wider world.

### Suggested political geography (provisional)

- **Imperial Capital:** seat of the sovereign, central ministries, high court, treasury and major academies.
- **Seven Ancestral Territories:** regions historically associated with each elemental family.
- **Free Cities:** commercial cities whose wealth and strategic location allow them to negotiate special autonomy.
- **Frontier Marches:** dangerous border regions near wild territories, ancient ruins and high-density mana zones.
- **Neutral Sanctuaries:** protected ancient libraries, healing grounds or treaty locations that no family may seize without broad consequences.

Exact names, geography, borders and the number of states remain undecided.

## 2. The Seven Great Families

Each family is more than a group of powerful individuals. It is a political house, military institution, knowledge tradition and economic network.

Each has a patriarch or matriarch, succession structure, main and collateral branches, affiliated clans, retainers, family beast species, breeding lines, cultivation methods, territory, businesses and political obligations. Each should also contain internal factions rather than acting as one unified mind.

### Proposed public roles

These are thematic starting points, not exclusive monopolies.

| Family | Public reputation / specialty | Possible influence |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | offensive force, forging, heat and transformation | forges, weapon production, furnace industries, assault units |
| Water | healing, adaptation, flow and support | medicine, water transport, irrigation, healing houses |
| Earth | defence, construction, stability and endurance | mining, fortifications, roads, buildings, defensive beasts |
| Wind | mobility, scouting, communication and travel | courier networks, air routes, exploration, reconnaissance |
| Lightning | speed, precision, energy and shock tactics | advanced mechanisms, signal networks, rapid-response forces |
| Light | public healing, purification, judgement and legitimacy | sanctuaries, hospitals, education, public ceremonies and moral authority |
| Dark | concealment, containment, absorption and poorly understood arts | historically linked to balancing or harmonizing elemental forces; current public influence is diminished |

These roles are not exclusive. A Water-affiliated merchant can trade weapons; an Earth family can operate hospitals. Their inherited expertise gives them advantages, not ownership of every related industry.

### Internal family factions

Each house can contain traditionalists, reformers, ambitious branches, pragmatic merchants, military hardliners, younger dissenters and ordinary members who simply want security. A future ally may come from a rival family's branch, while a Dark Family member may oppose the protagonist for understandable reasons.

## 3. Central political institutions

### The Imperial Throne

The sovereign is the formal head of the realm. The throne's legitimacy may come from founding history, control of the capital, recognition by the great houses and the ability to coordinate defence.

The throne should be powerful but constrained. It cannot casually order the seven families to surrender ancestral beasts, territories or techniques without consequences.

### The Seven Houses Council

**Proposal:** a council where each great family holds a hereditary seat. It negotiates inter-family disputes, territory and resource rights, military contributions, academy standards, restrictions on dangerous beasts or artifacts, emergency responses to mana-vein disasters and treaties with neighbouring states.

The council can be publicly described as a balance among the seven elements. In practice, the six allied houses may use their majority to pressure the Dark Family.

Do not make the council omnipotent. Its resolutions may require imperial ratification, local enforcement or family cooperation. Political leverage is more interesting than an institution that can command everyone without resistance.

### Imperial ministries and courts

Suggested institutions:
- **Ministry of Territories:** governors, city administration, land and infrastructure.
- **Ministry of Revenue:** taxes, tariffs, treasury and currency standards.
- **Ministry of Military Affairs:** national forces, border defence and mobilisation.
- **Ministry of Beasts and Mana Resources:** beast habitats, dangerous-species notices, resource permits and mana-vein oversight.
- **High Court:** disputes between families, guilds, cities and citizens.
- **Registry Office:** legal beast contracts, artifact ownership, licenses and major cultivation credentials.

Names are placeholders. The setting's cultural naming style can be chosen later.

### Law and enforcement

A workable legal order has several layers:
- civil and criminal law for citizens;
- family law for inheritance, marriage, branch status and succession, within state limits;
- beast-contract law for partnership obligations, injury, abandonment and disputes;
- resource law for hunting grounds, mana-vein access, protected habitats and core harvesting;
- artifact law for dangerous relics, restricted weapons and public safety.

The wealthy may influence enforcement, but law should still matter. Bribery, jurisdiction disputes, political immunity and selective enforcement create plot opportunities.

## 4. The Dark Family's position

The Dark Family should be a recognized great house whose status has been hollowed out, not a forgotten family nobody knows exists. Its decline is therefore visible and politically useful.

### Public position

- It retains its historic name and perhaps its council seat, but has lost influence.
- Its territory or resource access may have been reduced through treaties, debt, compulsory contributions or administrative decisions.
- It is expected to fulfil obligations imposed under the ancestral promise.
- Its members face prejudice because Dark is associated with danger, corruption or forbidden beasts.
- Traditional beast-breeding lines may be declining due to resource shortages and restrictions.
- Other houses publicly describe the restrictions as necessary safeguards.

### The six-family pressure system

Rather than having the other six simply attack whenever they wish, give their pressure a bureaucratic and economic form. Possible mechanisms include annual resource quotas, compulsory military or expeditionary contributions, unequal tariffs, mandatory provision of selected beasts or materials, limits on access to mana veins or breeding grounds, inspection rights justified as public safety, historical debt claims and council resolutions selectively enforced.

These are possible instruments, not confirmed obligations. The exact demands must be decided by the author.

### Why the Dark Family cannot simply refuse

The ancestral promise should have political consequences as well as mystical consequences:

1. **Oath consequence:** violating the promise triggers an unknown or known ancestral penalty.
2. **Political consequence:** refusal gives the six houses a public justification to isolate or sanction the Dark Family.
3. **Economic consequence:** trade, resource access and affiliated businesses can be cut off.
4. **Military consequence:** the weakened Dark Family cannot win a direct confrontation while outnumbered.
5. **Moral consequence:** the former head may have made the promise to prevent a catastrophe, so breaking it could endanger more than the family.

Keep the exact oath wording, enforcement mechanism and original reason unresolved until the author decides them.

### Internal Dark Family factions

- **Survival faction:** comply, preserve descendants and wait for a safer moment.
- **Restoration faction:** rebuild strength and negotiate from a stronger position.
- **Retaliation faction:** prepare to punish the six houses, even if it risks the oath.
- **Pragmatists:** seek trade, marriage alliances and external support.
- **Younger generation:** question why they must inherit humiliation from an old promise.

This creates conflict without making every elder foolish or cowardly.

## 5. Military power and security

Military strength is not measured only by the strongest individual beast tamer. A state needs logistics, command, supply, intelligence, healing, transport and trained beasts.

### Main military branches

| Branch | Role | Typical beast use |
|---|---|---|
| Imperial Field Army | large-scale warfare and territorial defence | infantry-support, siege, shield and assault beasts |
| Border Guard | protect frontier settlements and roads | tracking, patrol, endurance and ranged beasts |
| Aerial / Rapid Corps | fast movement, courier interception and response | flying, wind, lightning and high-mobility beasts |
| Beast Suppression Corps | contain stampedes and dangerous wild beasts | restraint, barrier, calming and heavy combat beasts |
| City Guard | law enforcement and public safety | tracking, detection, defensive and non-lethal control beasts |
| Expeditionary Corps | explore ruins, mana zones and dangerous territories | scouting, survival, sensing, hauling and combat beasts |
| Medical and Recovery Corps | battlefield treatment and evacuation | healing, cleansing, transport and protective beasts |

These are functional branches; each state may name and organize them differently.

### Family forces

Each great house maintains guards and specialist units. They protect family estates, breeding grounds, caravans, resource sites and political interests. Family troops may be more loyal to their house than to the throne, forcing the sovereign to negotiate.

### Mercenaries and independent companies

Mercenaries escort caravans, clear routes, explore dangerous territories, retrieve rare materials, hunt or capture specified beasts, guard mines and mana-vein sites, and provide temporary specialist teams.

Contracts should specify payment, salvage rights, casualty responsibility, beast-handling rules and ownership of recovered cores or artifacts.

### Military doctrine

A force may combine front-line defensive beasts, ranged attackers, scouts and trackers, aerial units, healers and support beasts, siege and terrain-control beasts, and supply and transport beasts.

A lower-ranked force with better coordination and terrain preparation can defeat a stronger but disorganized opponent. This prevents every battle from becoming a simple comparison of cultivation rank.

## 6. Academies, schools and training

### A layered education system

1. **Village and town schools:** literacy, arithmetic, basic mana safety, local beast knowledge and ordinary education.
2. **Regional cultivation institutes:** basic scripts, core development, affinity theory, beast care and supervised practical training.
3. **Family academies:** preserve family species knowledge, inherited techniques, history and specialist methods.
4. **Imperial academies:** train students from different regions and families; produce administrators, officers, researchers and elite beast tamers.
5. **Specialist institutions:** medicine, artifact crafting, beast breeding, mana-vein surveying, alchemy, law and exploration.

### Elite academy design

A prestigious academy can become a major story location, but it should not be the only path to power.

Possible features include admission by examination, recommendation, sponsorship or family nomination; scholarships for exceptional commoners; practical and theoretical evaluations; supervised beast-contract grounds; public libraries and restricted archives; field expeditions; inter-house competitions; student associations; political factions; and disciplinary rules for dangerous beast use.

### Academy politics

Students arrive with unequal resources. A child from a major house may have a family beast, private tutor and expensive mana crystals. A talented commoner may have ability but no access to high-quality scripts or materials.

This creates patronage, sponsorship, discrimination by family and affinity, competition for instructors, disputes over training resources, student alliances and recruitment attempts by powerful houses.

The Dark Family may have lost access to instructors, records or recognized training facilities. Its young members might attend institutions where Dark techniques are stigmatized or poorly taught. The protagonist should not have every door opened immediately; he may need to prove himself, find a mentor or uncover neglected knowledge.

## 7. Artifacts, equipment and crafted items

Artifacts support the mana economy and beast-taming professions. Each should have a function, limits, costs and maintenance needs.

### Proposed item categories

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Mana storage | crystals, cores, storage stones, sealed reservoirs | store or transport mana |
| Cultivation aids | breathing arrays, meditation mats, script tablets | improve safe mana accumulation |
| Beast-care tools | feeding vessels, grooming tools, recovery chambers | support health and training |
| Contract tools | contract seals, resonance bells, binding or consent arrays | assist a contract process, not replace compatibility or trust |
| Detection tools | affinity stones, core gauges, mana lenses | measure or reveal mana properties |
| Combat equipment | weapons, armor, talismans, traps | protect or enhance combat capability |
| Travel and utility | storage containers, communication tokens, transport arrays | support daily life and expeditions |
| Relics | ancient artifacts with unusual or poorly understood functions | preserve lost knowledge or create mysteries |

### Quality dimensions

Do not use one simplistic rank to describe everything. Assess items by:
- **Material quality:** what it is made from.
- **Mana capacity:** how much mana it can hold or channel.
- **Purity:** how contaminated or refined its mana is.
- **Stability:** whether it performs safely.
- **Affinity:** which elemental resonance it supports.
- **Craftsmanship:** precision, durability and repairability.
- **Rarity:** how difficult it is to obtain or reproduce.

An expensive item is not automatically useful to every cultivator. A refined Fire artifact may be unsuitable for a Water specialist.

### Artifact regulation

Common tools can be openly traded. Dangerous weapons, large-scale mana arrays, soul-affecting relics and devices that can destabilize a mana vein may require licenses or be restricted.

This creates black markets, smugglers, counterfeiters, inspectors and disputes over inherited relics.

The Dark Family may possess old artifacts misunderstood because modern craftspeople no longer understand their design. Not every ancient Dark relic should be supreme; some can be broken, incomplete, specialized or dangerous even to its owner.

## 8. Economy and trade

The economy must include ordinary labour and agriculture. Most people should not spend every day buying divine beast cores.

### Main economic sectors

Agriculture and livestock; mana-enhanced crops and medicinal plants; mining and mana-crystal extraction; beast breeding and husbandry; legal beast capture and rescue; beast-core processing; medicine and alchemy; artifact and equipment crafting; transport and logistics; construction and mana-vein infrastructure; education and healing; exploration and mercenary work.

### Currency

**Proposal:** ordinary state-issued currency supports daily life, while mana crystals and valuable materials are used for high-value trade.

A possible structure:
- copper or small-denomination coins for food, lodging and everyday goods;
- silver for skilled labour, ordinary equipment and local services;
- gold for property, business contracts and expensive training;
- certified mana crystals for major beast purchases, rare materials and inter-regional settlement.

Exact denominations, exchange rates and whether the realm uses coin, paper notes or stamped tokens remain open.

Mana crystals should not automatically function as perfect money. Their value depends on shape, size, density, purity, stability, elemental resonance, origin and verification. A merchant needs testing tools and standards to prevent fraud.

### Resource valuation

A market quote should distinguish raw crystal from refined crystal, common mana ore from rare deposits, stable cores from damaged or contaminated cores, elemental compatibility, legal provenance, extraction risk and transport cost.

A small crystal can be more valuable than a large but impure one.

### Trade routes and public finance

Important routes connect mining regions to refining towns, beast habitats to licensed markets, agricultural basins to cities, frontier outposts to supply centres, and academies and craft centres to rare-resource suppliers.

Control of a road, bridge, river crossing or mana-vein junction can be as politically important as control of a fortress.

The state may collect land and property taxes, market and trade tariffs, mining and mana-vein royalties, dangerous-beast capture licenses, artifact-crafting fees and expedition permits. A family can decline through lost mine access or punitive tariffs without a battle.

## 9. Beast markets and mercenaries

Beast markets are major economic institutions, not merely places where the protagonist buys a powerful creature.

### Main market channels

1. **Family breeding markets:** established species and bloodlines raised by trusted family-affiliated breeders.
2. **Licensed breeder markets:** independent breeders sell cultivated, trained or partially trained beasts.
3. **Wild-capture markets:** legally captured beasts from dangerous habitats, with provenance and health records.
4. **Rescue and rehoming exchanges:** beasts displaced by habitat destruction, failed contracts or owner death.
5. **Auction houses:** rare species, unusual bloodlines, high-potential juveniles and legally recovered relics.
6. **Black markets:** stolen beasts, protected species, forged pedigrees, illegal cores and dangerous contract methods.

### Why family members usually use family beasts

This follows the author's established direction:
- each major family has particular beast species with high compatibility with its members;
- generations of breeding and cultivation create reliable family lines;
- family members usually cultivate those established beasts rather than searching the wild;
- wild exploration is expensive, dangerous and uncertain;
- wild capture is a special decision, not the default route for ordinary family members.

A family beast is not necessarily the strongest possible beast. It is a dependable match supported by generations of knowledge, facilities, breeding records and care.

### Why wild beasts remain valuable

A wild beast may offer a rare species absent from family breeding grounds, unusual traits or an unrecorded mutation, a different bloodline branch, a compatibility match unavailable through ordinary markets, a special ability needed for a role, or a chance to establish a new breeding line.

Risks include injury, failed capture, incompatible temperament, hidden illness, unstable mana, expensive transport and uncertain evolution potential.

### Mercenary role

Mercenary companies locate rare beasts, survey habitats, escort breeders and buyers, capture specified species, rescue injured or displaced beasts, verify wild-capture provenance, protect breeding expeditions and recover lost beasts and cargo.

Contracts distinguish the mercenary fee from ownership of the captured beast. A buyer may pay a fixed capture fee, success bonus and separate purchase price. Salvage rights and injury liability should be agreed beforehand.

### Market safeguards

A reputable market provides species and origin identification, age and health assessment, current core condition, known affinity and abilities, temperament report, contract history where legally available, capture or breeding provenance, and known risks and care requirements.

No test guarantees a beast's future potential. Fraudulent sellers may exaggerate bloodlines or hide injuries.

## 10. Social life and class structure

The world should contain ordinary people with jobs, families, ambitions and concerns unrelated to becoming the strongest beast tamer.

### Suggested social groups

| Group | Typical position |
|---|---|
| Sovereign and royal household | formal state authority and court politics |
| Great families and major clans | hereditary influence, territory and private resources |
| Officials and administrators | law, taxation, city management and public works |
| Elite beast tamers | military, exploration, security and high-value services |
| Merchants and craft guilds | trade, finance, production and logistics |
| Academics and specialists | education, research, medicine and artifact work |
| Independent cultivators | self-funded practitioners and itinerant professionals |
| Farmers, labourers and artisans | food, construction, manufacture and essential services |
| Frontier settlers | high-risk communities near wild habitats and resource zones |
| Servants, retainers and bonded workers | household and institutional labour; legal status needs careful definition |

Do not assume every person is a beast tamer. Many citizens may have only ordinary mana development or no meaningful cultivation career.

### Everyday use of beasts

Beasts can haul carts and agricultural equipment, guard farms and storehouses, detect fires or intruders, carry messages, assist construction, support medicine and recovery, transport people and goods, and help manage water, soil and crops.

Rare combat beasts should not be casually used for errands. Common utility species can be domesticated and bred for work.

### Urban design

Mana-rich cities may have public mana lamps or low-cost lighting arrays, beast stables and care yards, regulated market districts, transport stations, public wells and water systems, healing clinics, mana-crystal assay offices, designated training grounds and protected habitats for non-aggressive species.

Dangerous beasts should not wander freely through crowded residential districts. Cities need containment rules and emergency response procedures.

### Family, marriage and festivals

Affinity, family reputation, beast bloodlines, inheritance and cultivation resources can influence marriage arrangements. Affection, personal choice, class and political alliances should all matter; not every marriage needs to be arranged.

The concubine hierarchy in the protagonist's household can reflect elite customs, but not every region or social class must follow the same structure.

Possible public events include seasonal beast fairs, harvest celebrations, ancestor ceremonies, academy tournaments, city founding days, mana-vein inspection festivals, public beast parades and remembrance ceremonies for frontier defenders.

The seven elements can influence regional art, architecture, clothing, food and ceremony without reducing every person to an elemental stereotype.

## 11. Information, reputation and public morality

Because the novel contrasts apparently evil Dark with apparently righteous but corrupted Light, control of information must be part of the world order.

### Institutions that shape public belief

Schools teach simplified official history; sanctuaries or public moral institutions interpret Light and Dark; family historians preserve selective ancestral records; merchants spread news through trade routes; bounty notices and beast registries shape perceptions of dangerous species; courts determine which historical claims are legally recognized; academies decide which techniques are respectable or forbidden.

### Possible Light institutional influence

The Light Family may have a respected public role in healing, purification and moral judgement. Its influence becomes dangerous if it also controls which records are copied and taught, which beasts are labelled corrupted, which cultivation methods are forbidden, who may study ancient ruins and which public accusations are accepted as truth.

This is a proposed avenue for institutional corruption, not a declaration that every Light-affiliated person is malicious.

### Counterweights

Include independent scholars, rival historians, merchants who value evidence, local communities with memories contradicting official accounts, neutral archives, former officials and defectors, and beasts whose behaviour challenges accepted doctrine.

The protagonist should uncover truth through evidence, relationships and consequences, not one convenient exposition speech.

## 12. Frontier, exploration and the wider world

A beast-taming civilization needs places beyond safe cities.

### Proposed frontier zones

- **Managed habitats:** monitored areas where common beasts live and licensed capture is permitted.
- **Dangerous wild territories:** habitats where powerful beasts, mana storms or unstable terrain make expeditions costly.
- **Mana-vein regions:** valuable zones where extraction, settlement and ecological stability compete.
- **Ancient ruins:** places containing relics, lost scripts, old records and unknown dangers.
- **Forbidden or sealed regions:** areas restricted because of historical disasters, dangerous species or political agreements.

Frontier exploration is a profession requiring preparation, maps, guides, supplies, medical support, risk-sharing and contracts—not simply a walk into a forest.

A state may claim territory but lack strength to secure it. A family may possess an ancestral right but lack funds to exploit it. A mercenary company may know the routes better than the governor. These overlapping claims create believable conflict.

## 13. Reusable plot engines

These are possible conflict generators, not predetermined events.

1. **Quota dispute:** the six houses demand beasts or mana resources the weakened Dark Family cannot safely provide.
2. **Trade blockade:** a merchant alliance quietly cuts off supplies to a family branch.
3. **Academy discrimination:** a student is denied access to a technique or training ground because of elemental prejudice.
4. **Wild-capture expedition:** a rare beast appears, and factions compete to fund or sabotage the expedition.
5. **Counterfeit core scandal:** market fraud threatens trust in the beast trade and implicates a powerful house.
6. **Mana-vein crisis:** over-extraction or damage threatens a city, forcing rivals to cooperate.
7. **Artifact ownership dispute:** an ancient relic is claimed by the state, an academy and a family.
8. **Military requisition:** the Dark Family is ordered to provide forces despite lacking resources.
9. **Historical trial:** evidence about the betrayal emerges, but the official court is politically compromised.
10. **Frontier settlement conflict:** settlers, beast habitats, mercenaries and family resource rights collide.
11. **Light institution scandal:** a respected institution's policy is exposed as serving political control rather than public welfare.
12. **Internal succession struggle:** a Dark Family faction disagrees over whether to comply, reform or resist.

## 14. Continuity rules for future AI chapter writers

1. The seven great elemental families are major powers, but are not the only political, military or economic institutions.
2. Treat the imperial realm and Seven Houses Council as proposals until the author confirms the exact government.
3. Do not make the six families omnipotent; they rely on institutions, resources, alliances and enforcement.
4. The Dark Family is diminished and pressured, but exact quotas, debts, territory losses and legal restrictions remain undecided.
5. Keep the ancestral promise distinct from ordinary political treaties unless the author defines their relationship.
6. Military strength includes logistics, coordination, intelligence, healing, transport and supply, not just individual beast rank.
7. Mercenaries operate through contracts; fees, salvage, capture ownership and liability should be clear.
8. Academies are one route to training, not the only route. Family instruction, regional institutes, private mentors and independent study can exist.
9. Artifacts have functions, limits, compatibility, maintenance and provenance. Not every ancient relic is overwhelmingly powerful.
10. Mana crystals and cores have variable value based on quality and properties; size alone does not determine price.
11. Ordinary currency supports daily trade; high-value mana materials may be used in major transactions. Exact currency names and exchange rates are undecided.
12. Family-bred beasts are the normal, reliable route for most family members. Wild-beast acquisition is expensive, risky and comparatively uncommon.
13. Beast markets include breeders, licensed wild-capture traders, auctions, rescue exchanges and illegal channels.
14. Most citizens are not elite beast tamers. Preserve ordinary livelihoods and social differences.
15. Light and Dark are elemental resonances, not automatic moral categories.
16. Do not assume every Light institution is corrupt or every Dark character is virtuous.
17. New laws, institutions, military ranks, currencies, artifact classes and political events introduced during drafting must be proposed for author approval.

## 15. Decisions still required

### Government and geography
- Is the setting one empire, several kingdoms, a federation or a continent with multiple states?
- Does the emperor rule by bloodline, conquest, mandate or agreement among the houses?
- Does the Seven Houses Council have legal, advisory or treaty-based authority?
- How many regions and major cities are needed for the first arcs?

### Seven families
- What are the official names of the seven houses?
- What species does each family traditionally breed and cultivate?
- What territories and resources does each house control?
- Which family leads the anti-Dark alliance, and which members privately disagree?

### Military
- Does the throne maintain a national army separate from family forces?
- Can family forces operate outside their territories?
- What laws govern beast use in cities and warfare?
- Is service mandatory or voluntary?

### Academies
- At what age do children commonly enter regional or elite academies?
- Is the protagonist expected to attend an academy?
- Are family academies open to outsiders?
- How are scholarships, examinations and student contracts handled?

### Economy and daily life
- What are the currency names and exchange standards?
- Are mana crystals widely accepted as money or only for high-value trade?
- How are beast ownership, capture permits and market inspections regulated?
- What is the ordinary standard of living in villages, cities and frontier settlements?

### Artifacts and law
- Are artifacts graded by rank, quality, function or a combination?
- Which artifacts require licenses?
- Can artifacts be inherited, registered, sold or confiscated?
- Who controls ancient ruins and recovered relics?

## 16. Author-facing design recommendation

For the opening portion of the novel, reveal the world order through the protagonist's lived experience rather than a textbook explanation.

1. **Household:** show how the patriarch's children, concubine branches and family resources are distributed.
2. **Family:** reveal the Dark Family's obligations and declining influence through a concrete demand.
3. **Local society:** show how ordinary merchants, neighbours and officials treat Dark-affiliated people.
4. **Academy or market:** demonstrate how affinity, family pedigree and beast compatibility affect opportunity.
5. **Regional politics:** reveal that the six houses coordinate pressure through councils, laws and economic mechanisms.
6. **Wider world:** gradually introduce the throne, military, frontier and ancient institutions.

Keep exposition attached to conflict and character decisions.

**Core design principle:** power in this world is not only the ability to defeat an enemy. It is also the ability to control resources, shape laws, train the next generation, define public truth, command trade routes and decide which people are allowed to grow strong.
