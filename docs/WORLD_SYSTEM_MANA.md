# World System 01 — Mana, Cores and Planetary Veins

**Status: Working proposal — not fully canon-locked.**

This document records the mana-system discussion and separates the author's established foundations from proposed extensions. Future chapter-writing agents must treat items marked "proposal" as provisional until the author confirms them. Do not silently turn open questions into canon.

## 1. Foundational premises from the author

- Mana exists naturally throughout the environment.
- Mana can be found in multiple physical forms, including round forms, spheres, diamonds, crystals and ore.
- Humans naturally absorb mana through ordinary breathing and bodily life processes. Over time, mana accumulates and develops into an internal Mana Core.
- An ordinary human may take approximately 70 years to develop a complete first core through natural, unassisted development.
- Beast tamers recite a special script that acts as a catalyst, accelerating mana accumulation and core development.
- Beasts and animals also possess mana cores.
- Plants interact with mana and can produce mana-bearing cores, crystals or other condensed forms.
- The planet itself has mana veins. The author wants the planetary origin and scale of mana to have a cosmological foundation comparable in scope to a Big Bang origin story.
- Human core layers and beast core ranks are related and should use a coherent progression system.

## 2. Proposed cosmology: primordial and natural mana

**Proposal:** Mana predates the world. In the earliest cosmic era, primordial mana existed in an unrefined state. A universe-shaping event caused concentrations of this energy to gather and condense. The planet formed with an immense concentration of primordial mana embedded deep within it.

Distinguish two broad states:

- **Primordial Mana:** ancient, extremely dense, difficult to interact with safely and associated with the earliest cosmic era.
- **Natural Mana:** mana released, diluted and circulated through the world; the form most ordinary organisms can absorb.

This is a proposed explanation, not yet a confirmed historical account. The actual event, its name, whether it was truly an explosion, and whether anyone in the current era knows the truth remain undecided.

## 3. Planetary mana veins

**Proposal:** Treat the planet as a mana-circulating system. Mana veins are immense natural channels through which deep planetary mana moves and gradually feeds the environment.

Suggested hierarchy:

1. **Planetary Mana Source/Core:** the deepest concentration or origin point of the world's mana circulation.
2. **Major Mana Veins:** enormous channels crossing continental or regional distances.
3. **Minor Mana Veins:** branches feeding local mountains, forests, rivers, plains and underground deposits.
4. **Mana-rich environments:** places where mana seeps into air, water, soil and living ecosystems.

Potential world consequences:
- Regions above rich veins may support stronger beasts, unusual plants and better cultivation.
- Mana-poor regions may experience weaker growth, declining beast populations or difficulty cultivating.
- Damaged, blocked or unstable veins may cause local disasters or abnormal mana phenomena.
- Access to mana veins can become a source of territorial, economic and political conflict.
- A rare leak of primordial mana could create a dangerous forbidden region.

These consequences are development suggestions, not established events.

## 4. Mana in living and non-living matter

A shared underlying energy connects humans, beasts, plants, minerals and the planet. However, the structures that hold mana should not all be called a Mana Core.

| Term | Proposed meaning |
|---|---|
| **Mana Core** | An organized internal energy structure in a human or beast. |
| **Mana Crystal** | Mana condensed into a stable crystalline material. |
| **Mana Sphere / Orb** | A rounded, condensed or refined form of mana-bearing material. |
| **Mana Diamond** | A highly compressed, orderly mana-bearing formation. |
| **Mana Ore** | Raw mineral deposits containing mana. |
| **Mana Node** | A concentrated mana structure developed by a plant or unusual organism. |
| **Mana Cluster** | Multiple crystals or mana-bearing formations grown together. |

Plants absorb mana through their roots, leaves and surrounding environment. Depending on species, age and habitat, they may form mana nodes, crystal fruits, resin, seeds or other condensed deposits. The exact biological categories remain open.

Minerals can accumulate or trap mana over geological periods. They are not necessarily alive and should not automatically be described as possessing a living core.

### Evaluating mana materials

**Proposal:** Assess materials using four independent qualities:

- **Density:** quantity of mana held in a given volume.
- **Purity:** proportion of usable mana versus impurities or unstable residue.
- **Affinity:** elemental resonance, if any.
- **Stability:** how safely the mana can be stored, transported or consumed.

Shape alone does not determine quality. A small, pure and stable sphere may be more valuable than a large, contaminated crystal.

## 5. Human Mana Core development

### Natural development

Ordinary humans unconsciously absorb ambient mana through breathing and normal bodily processes. Their bodies gradually accumulate and organize this energy into an internal core.

The author's current baseline is approximately 70 years for an ordinary person to develop a complete first core naturally. This is a general benchmark, not yet a precisely defined biological law. Individual health, environment, age of exposure and natural aptitude may eventually be relevant, but their effects need author approval.

### Beast-tamer development

Beast tamers recite a cultivation script that acts as a catalyst. The script does not create mana from nothing. Its proposed functions are to:
- improve the body's absorption of ambient mana;
- help organize accumulated mana into a core;
- reduce wasted or dispersed energy;
- support core formation and subsequent refinement;
- establish or strengthen resonance with elemental mana.

The exact physical and metaphysical mechanism remains to be designed.

## 6. Core formation and layers

**Proposal:** Distinguish the formation of a core from the advancement of a completed core.

Suggested early progression:
1. **Core Seed:** mana begins gathering into a stable internal nucleus.
2. **Core Formation:** the nucleus becomes a functional Mana Core.
3. **Layer One:** the first complete structural layer is formed.
4. **Layer Two:** a second layer is completed.
5. **Layer Three and beyond:** additional completed layers represent higher cultivation ranks.

The layer count should be the shared language for comparing human cultivation and beast rank. A human at a given layer and a beast at the corresponding rank have comparable core development, but this does not guarantee equal combat strength. Species, body, abilities, experience, affinity, techniques and environment can produce different outcomes.

A layer should represent a completed structural advancement, not merely a temporary quantity of stored mana. The number of layers, names for higher ranks, breakthrough requirements, lifespan effects and failure risks remain open.

## 7. Cultivation scripts

**Proposal:** A script is an ordered recitation or sequence that creates resonance between the cultivator's body and ambient mana. It may combine spoken patterns, breathing rhythm, concentration and mana circulation.

Possible script categories:
- **Common Script:** basic method available to ordinary practitioners.
- **Family Script:** inherited or guarded method developed by a clan or family.
- **Elemental Script:** method optimized for a particular elemental resonance.
- **Ancient Script:** rare, lost or partially understood method.
- **Primordial Script:** speculative method capable of interacting with primordial mana.

Do not assume all categories exist in the current world until established in later discussion.

### Possible Dark Family mystery

A future plot possibility is that the Dark Family's original cultivation knowledge was damaged, fragmented, restricted or deliberately misrepresented by the other six families. The surviving Dark method may be incomplete, and its true function may be broader than the current world believes.

This is only a mystery candidate. It is not yet confirmed that the six families destroyed the script, that the Dark Family has a unique primordial script, or that the protagonist will discover it.

## 8. The seven elemental resonances

The seven major elements remain the world's established framework: **Dark, Light, Fire, Water, Earth, Wind and Lightning.**

**Proposal:** These are seven resonances or expressions of the same underlying mana, rather than seven unrelated substances.

Possible thematic and functional directions:
- **Fire:** heat, combustion, transformation.
- **Water:** flow, adaptation, restoration.
- **Earth:** foundation, endurance, structure.
- **Wind:** movement, speed, freedom.
- **Lightning:** energy, acceleration, penetration.
- **Light:** revelation, illumination, purification.
- **Dark:** concealment, absorption, depth and the unknown.

These are starting points, not final ability lists. Elements must not be treated as moral alignments. Light is not inherently good and Dark is not inherently evil. This supports the novel's intended inversion: apparently righteous Light institutions may be corrupt, while the stigmatized Dark Family may have protective or honorable motives.

The relationship between elemental affinity, core formation, beast compatibility and the ability to cultivate multiple elements needs further discussion.

## 9. The natural mana cycle

**Proposal:** Mana moves through a broad cycle:

1. Deep planetary mana feeds the planet's veins.
2. Mana seeps into the environment, including air, water and soil.
3. Humans, beasts and plants absorb it.
4. Living organisms organize some of it into cores or specialized mana structures.
5. Some mana is released through ordinary life processes, injury, death and decay.
6. Some remains condensed in cores, bones, blood, seeds, plants and minerals.
7. Over long periods, mana returns to the environment or becomes part of geological deposits.

Not all mana returns immediately after an organism dies. This provides a possible basis for beast-core harvesting, alchemy, materials, trade and ecological consequences. Exact rules for harvesting, contamination, regeneration and conservation remain open.

## 10. Story and worldbuilding opportunities

These are possible consequences to explore, not current canon:
- States and powerful families compete for territory above major mana veins.
- Mana-rich forests and mountains become valuable habitats and dangerous frontiers.
- Beast cores become valuable resources, creating tension between hunting, conservation and cultivation.
- Plant-based mana products support medicine, food, crafting and commerce.
- Mana crystal quality standards create professions, markets, fraud and social inequality.
- A damaged vein can threaten a city or region and create a conflict larger than a personal duel.
- Ancient ruins may preserve knowledge about primordial mana and the original planetary system.
- The Dark Family's history may involve a misunderstanding or suppressed truth about the seven resonances.

## 11. Rules for future AI chapter writers

1. Do not invent new cultivation ranks, core-layer counts, script names or mana laws as established facts without author approval.
2. Keep primordial mana distinct from ordinary natural mana unless a later canon decision changes this.
3. Do not treat every mana-bearing object as a living Mana Core.
4. Do not equate material size with quality; density, purity, affinity and stability are separate properties.
5. Do not assume elemental affinity determines morality.
6. Do not assume corresponding human and beast ranks guarantee equal combat ability.
7. Treat the 70-year natural human core-development period as the current general benchmark.
8. Preserve all unresolved items as questions or clearly labelled theories.
9. When a chapter introduces a new mana-system rule, flag it as a proposed canon change for the author to approve.
10. When confirmed, update the relevant canon, world-state, object, timeline and knowledge registries as appropriate.

## 12. Open design questions

The author should decide these before the system is locked:

- Is the first completed Mana Core a single structure, or does it already contain a first layer?
- How many layers exist in each major rank?
- Does every human have one elemental affinity, multiple affinities or initially neutral mana?
- Can a person change or expand their elemental affinity?
- Are cultivation scripts spoken words, ancient phonetic patterns, mental recitations or a combination?
- Why does the script accelerate core formation, and what limits its use?
- Can a human cultivate without a script, and can a beast tamer use another person's script?
- Do beasts develop cores at the same rate as humans, or according to species and age?
- Are beast cores harvestable, and what happens to the beast when its core is removed?
- Do plants produce living cores, crystals, fruits or several distinct products?
- Can mana crystals recharge, or are they finite once consumed?
- Can mana veins be depleted, damaged, redirected or permanently destroyed?
- What exactly was the cosmic event that created the planet's mana system?
- What is the true relationship between Dark mana and the other six elements?

The detailed cosmology, terminology, material properties, cultivation stages, cycle and plot hooks above are structured proposals pending approval.
